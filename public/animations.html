Motion notes

  • Deal · 3-2-kitty From dealer (top-right) to each seat: 3 cards around, then 2 around, then 3 to the kitty. 333ms per card, 40ms gap, slight arc (y peaks ~20px mid-flight), +2° wobble on landing, fan offset of 6px per card at each seat.
    23 cards · ≈ 7.7s · easeOutQuad
  • Play Card lifts 6px + scales 1.04 on pickup (60ms), then arcs to its seat position on the felt; flips face-up between frame 5 and 8, lands with a 2px settle-bounce. Trump cards get a single pulse of the gold rim.
    450ms · easeOutCubic
  • Trick sweep Winner's card holds a 150ms glow (maple rim), then all four slide to the winner's seat on staggered 40ms offsets, flipping to face-down en route, scaling to 0.6 as they stack. Score tick chimes at end.
    900ms · ease-in-out
